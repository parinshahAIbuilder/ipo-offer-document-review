# Regulatory map

Every material finding carries a citation. Cite in this order of authority, and
give the most specific level you can actually support:

> Act -> Regulation or Section -> Rule -> Schedule -> Circular or Master
> Circular -> Accounting Standard -> Paragraph

A citation to "SEBI ICDR Regulations" alone is not a citation. "SEBI ICDR 2018
Schedule VI Part A clause 9(C)" is. If you cannot get to paragraph level,
consider whether you actually have a finding.

Verify any provision you are unsure of rather than citing from memory. Schedule
numbering in particular is easy to misremember, and a wrong schedule number in a
letter to an issuer is embarrassing in a way a hedged finding is not.

**KPI industry standards.** Circular SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-2/CIR/2025/28 dated
February 28, 2025, read with the ICAI Technical Guide on KPI Disclosures

definitions, and explanation of material movements. Check the resolution date
and the certificate are both disclosed.

**Rules that recur**

- Companies (Accounts) Rules 2014 Rule 3(1) and 3(5): audit trail, daily backup
  of books maintained in electronic form, server located in India.
- Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules 2014 Rule 11(g): auditor reporting on
  audit trail.
- Companies (Acceptance of Deposits) Rules 2014 Rule 2(1)(c)(xii): advance for
  supply of goods or services is excluded from deposits only if appropriated
  against supply within 365 days. Rule 16 and Form DPT 3 for the return.
- Companies (Meetings of Board and its Powers) Rules 2014 Rule 15: thresholds
  for related party approvals, including office or place of profit.

## Schedule III Division II

Mandatory items that are routinely absent or incomplete. Check each:

- Capital work in progress and intangible assets under development: ageing and
  completion schedules, including projects overdue or exceeding cost.
- Trade receivable and trade payable ageing schedules, with the six prescribed
  buckets and separate disclosure of undisputed and disputed.
- The eleven prescribed ratios, with explanation of any variance beyond 25 per
  cent against the prior year.
- Additional regulatory information: title deeds not held in the company's
  name, revaluation of property plant and equipment, loans to promoters and
  related parties repayable on demand, benami property, borrowings against
  current assets and quarterly returns filed with banks, wilful defaulter
  status, relationships with struck off companies, registration of charges and
  satisfaction with the Registrar, compliance with layers of companies, scheme
  of arrangement, utilisation of borrowed funds and share premium including
  ultimate beneficiaries, undisclosed income, and crypto currency transactions.
- Dues to micro and small enterprises shown separately on the face of the
  balance sheet, with the MSMED Act section 22 disclosures.

Benchmarks worth carrying: maximum domestic corporate rate 34.944 per cent;
section 115BAA effective rate 25.168 per cent. An effective rate above the
former implies permanent disallowances, foreign tax, or unrecognised deferred
tax on losses, and is worth a query.

## SEBI LODR Regulations 2015

Applies on listing rather than to the offer document, but findings about
governance readiness should cite it: Reg 16(1)(b) independence criteria, Reg 17
board composition, Reg 18 audit committee, Reg 23 related party transactions
and material RPT approval, Reg 24 subsidiary governance, Reg 30 and Schedule III
material event disclosure, Reg 33 financial results.
